Neil Golson

Carolina Athletics, Chief Marketing Officer

Written for Dany Berghoff, CAA Executive Search · October 2026

Short version

I'm VP of Brand and Creative at Realtor.com. Coca-Cola, Tesla and FlashParking before that, and a Home Depot store manager with a $30M P&L before any of it.

I grew up in Charlotte and graduated from Carolina in journalism and political science. My dad ran admissions and financial aid at Auburn and I got my MBA there, so I've watched SEC schools up close for almost thirty years.

I've spent 25 years on the buy side of the sponsorship table, deciding what to pay for an audience and what it's worth. I want this job.

What I think the job is

Carolina never had to build a mainstream fan. Dean Smith and Michael Jordan did it for us. So the base we have is the base that already loves us, and it thins out when football looks shaky. Basketball carries November to April, then it goes quiet until Omaha.

That's a product problem before it's a people problem. The growth is in millions of people who've never set foot in Chapel Hill.

Carolina has 750 student athletes and nobody telling their stories at scale. That's a content business sitting inside an athletics department. It's where I'd start.

Four proofs

Coca-Cola Brand and innovation

VAULT was chasing Mountain Dew and losing. I gave it a lane nobody else was in: Americana. Montgomery Gentry and the USO, camouflage cans in convenience stores, two years as title sponsor of the CMA Music Festival with the four-day event cut into a one-night special on ABC. I ran Coke USA's London Olympics across packaging, athlete programs, in-store merchandising and media, and did the same at the Final Four and NASCAR. First U.S. share growth in seven years.

Tesla Brand in the physical world

I owned how the brand showed up: the website, the showrooms, the service centers, the Supercharger destinations. Walk into one showroom for a test drive and we knew you on every screen after that. Field organization of 1,500.

FlashParking Category creation

Wrote the category, then took it from under 1% share to roughly a third of the market. Vista Equity put in $300M at a $1.1B valuation. Still private.

Realtor.com Current role

Homes.com was outspending us ten to one, Super Bowl included, and we were about to lose second place. I quit fighting on their ground and went at what we already had. I built the business case for Reba McEntire, negotiated the contract and services, pitched her the creative, hired the agency, and made six TV spots and a jingle. The top spot pulls 24.6% better response than what it replaced. Brand spend efficiency is up 20.8% year over year. Incremental leads now come from higher-value homes, a number that hadn't moved in a decade. Seven straight quarters of revenue growth against that outspend. I just re-signed her for two more years at half the price of the first term.

How I'd build the seat

Next to revenue and strategy, not under either one. Owning demand:

  • Brand, creative and content
  • Social, digital and production
  • Communications and broadcast
  • Fan experience and game presentation
  • Merchandising
  • The audience data under all of it

Measure me in merchandise moved, partners signed and renewed, and fans still wearing Carolina in July.

The gap

I've never worked inside a college athletics department. My read is that the commercial instinct is the scarce part and the campus mechanics are learnable, and that the right first hire under me is a deputy who already has them. You should push on that, because I would.
