The calendar's loaded. Every villa still sells at one price.

Since October, February opened up, the December minimum came off and Airbnb caught up. The rate is still one number per night across every unit we have.

Why three nights is the number that matters

Our owner weeks run Friday to Friday, 4pm in and 10am out. Housekeeping cleans every unit on Friday for a fixed fee whatever the stay, and can't come another day.

A Friday-to-Monday guest rides a cleaning we're buying anyway and is out four days before the next one. March is spring-break demand, the month that should take the long weekend most easily.

The askFive questions for management

  1. Who sets the nightly rate, and why is it the same for every unit on every date? Eight of ours are open this weekend at $162 while the private villas beside them sell at $121 to $138.
  2. Trailing twelve months, by unit: nights available to rent, nights booked, nights blocked for owner use, average daily rate, gross rental revenue. The October brief asked for it by December 1, and it's still what tells a sold night from a closed one.
  3. Who set the March minimum, and what would it cost to make it three nights? Friday is a turn day we already pay for.
  4. Easter week shows none of our 24. Sold, held by owners, or never released?
  5. Unit 590 is open on Heritage in December, February and March and missing from Airbnb every time. Is it connected?

One and two decide the rest.

How this was checked

Every Heritage search ran on book.heritagevacations.com on Tuesday, November 17, 2026, for two guests, no filters beyond the dates shown, reading every results page. Availability and rates move by the hour, so these counts are a snapshot and the links are live. Rates are the nightly figure Heritage shows before taxes and fees. October figures come from the October 9 brief.

Airbnb units were found by searching a tight map box over the property for the same dates, reading every results page, and matching each listing's title to a unit number. That finds what a guest can find. Airbnb totals include its own fees, so its prices aren't compared with Heritage here.

A public booking site can't tell a night that sold from a night that was never released, which is why question two exists.

The Friday turnover, the fixed housekeeping fee and the single cleaning day come from board and owner knowledge. The website does not show them.
