Spicebush at Sea Pines · Board brief

Nine empty villas on Halloween weekend

All nine priced at $185. Three weeks out. And by February we have nothing left to sell at all.

Neil Golson, Board of Directors Verified Friday, October 9, 2026

We have two problems, and they point in opposite directions. Close in, units sit open at one flat price. Further out, there's nothing in the calendar to work.

Nine of our 24 villas are bookable for Halloween weekend, every one at $185 a night. By mid-January we're down to one. By February, zero.

Every link opens the live booking site in a new tab. Run any of these yourself. The numbers move as inventory sells or gets loaded, which is why every number here is a link you can run.

Why three nights is the number that matters

Our owner weeks run Friday to Friday, 4pm in and 10am out. Housekeeping cleans every unit on Friday for a fixed fee whatever the stay, and can't come another day. The Heritage listing pages carry those same times.

Friday is a turn day we already pay for. A Friday-to-Monday guest rides a cleaning we're buying anyway and is out four days before the next one. That puts the marginal cost near zero and makes the long weekend the easiest sale we have. The four-night minimum takes it off the table for the winter.

The askFive questions for management

  1. Nine units are open on Halloween weekend at $185 each. What's the plan to fill them, and who sets that number? One price across nine villas on a holiday weekend isn't pricing.
  2. Trailing twelve months, by unit, delivered by December 1: nights available to rent, nights booked, nights blocked for owner use, average daily rate, gross rental revenue. Without it the board can't tell a sold-out night from a closed one.
  3. Through what date are Spicebush calendars and rates loaded? We show four units in December, one in January, none in February or March, while the private villas sell March at $283 to $479.
  4. Who set the four-night minimum starting December 1, and what would it cost to make it three? Friday is a turn day we already pay for.
  5. All 24 units are listed on Airbnb. Where does the availability gap come from? Halloween weekend, Heritage shows nine of ours bookable and an Airbnb map search returns two. November 20 to 23, Heritage shows five and Airbnb returns one.

One and two decide the rest.

How this was checked

Every search ran on book.heritagevacations.com on Friday, October 9, 2026, for two guests, no filters beyond the dates shown. Dates before October 16 are left out on purpose, since the board is on property this week and current occupancy says nothing useful. Availability and rates move by the hour, so these counts are a snapshot and the links are live.

The Airbnb units were found by searching a tight map box over the property across three date windows and checking each result for our description text, since Airbnb now shows its own generated titles in the search grid. That method finds what a guest can find. Management has since confirmed all 24 are listed, so these counts measure what shows up and can be booked on a given date.

Two limits worth stating. A public booking site can't tell a night that sold from a night that was never released, which is why question two exists. And February and March were tested at four and seven nights, so the zero for Spicebush in those months is a real absence of inventory. The minimum stay is not hiding it.

The Friday turnover, the fixed housekeeping fee and the single cleaning day come from board and owner knowledge. The website does not show them.
