The plan

First 90 days. Month 6. Month 12.

Specific, sequenced, and biased toward action, but front-loaded with listening so the moves are real, not imported.

First 90 days
Listen. Audit. Diagnose.
  • Listen to leadership, operators, field teams, and Players.
  • Audit acquisition, CRM, pricing, loyalty, local activation, and the events pipeline.
  • Identify the biggest friction points in first visit, repeat visit, and group/event sales.
  • Walk every state in the US footprint, one venue minimum, across dayparts, day-of-week, and group formats. ~30 states at one venue per ~3 days, with leadership immersion running parallel in weeks 1–2.
  • Ship occasion capture and the player invite in one region, with a control.
  • Stand up a single weekly operating cadence around invite completion, known-player share, off-peak occupancy, revenue per visit and 30-day rebook.
By month 6
Launch. Sharpen. Test.
  • Launch two growth plays: knowing every player at the front door, and switching the lifecycle on.
  • Sharpen segmentation and messaging: Player-first, with a discrete golfer track.
  • Test membership, loyalty, and frequency programs (PlayMore tiers, corporate variant).
  • Align brand, digital, venue, and CRM around repeatable playbooks.
  • Local marketing live: ten regional marketing directors running a national system against local numbers, selling a corporate-events product with event sales.
  • A new-news calendar: a reason to come back every season.
  • Offers sized to player value.
By month 12
Compound. Scale. Operate.
  • Improve frequency, utilization, revenue per bay, and repeat visits, measurably.
  • Build a scalable marketing operating system that runs across all venues.
  • Make Topgolf feel less like an occasional outing and more like a recurring social sports habit.
  • Convert the CDP and Toast layer into an adaptive yield + retention engine, built on one golden record.
  • Toptracer shot-back, with one golf tip, live in the bay.
  • Every lever tied to the EBITDA bridge and membership revenue, reported to the board each quarter.
  • Hand the CEO a brand and demand machine that compounds without needing a new venue.
